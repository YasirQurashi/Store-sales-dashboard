# Sales Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Overview

This document provides an analysis of key sales performance data for a mobile phone retailer operating across four cities.

Metrics reviewed include sales, units sold, orders, product performance, city-wise distribution, revenue trends, and salesperson performance.

The analysis addresses the following objectives:

- Evaluating overall sales performance through total sales, units, and orders
- Calculating product-wise performance across five phone brands
- Analyzing sales distribution by city
- Identifying monthly revenue trends
- Ranking salesperson performance
- Delivering actionable recommendations to sales stakeholders

The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Excel for data visualization.

## Dataset

This dataset covers sales transactions across a full calendar year (Jan–Dec), spanning five products, four cities/regions, and multiple salespersons.

Key metrics include:

- **Total Sales**: The overall revenue generated from all transactions.
- **Total Units**: The total number of individual product units sold.
- **Total Orders**: The count of distinct sales orders placed.
- **Product Performance**: Sales broken down by phone model — iPhone, Redmi, Samsung, Techno, and Vivo.
- **City Wise Sales**: The share of total sales attributable to each city — Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, and Sialkot.
- **Month Wise Revenue Trends**: Revenue tracked by month to identify seasonal patterns.
- **Top Salesperson Performance**: Revenue generated by the top five salespersons.

The dataset also allows filtering by **Product**, **Region**, and **Month**, enabling drill-down analysis at the city, product, or time-period level.

The dataset met a high standard of accuracy, and no further data cleaning was needed.

## Analysis

I began by importing the sales dataset and reviewing its structure and contents.

**Overall performance metrics** for the period were as follows:

- Total Sales: $28M
- Total Units: 777
- Total Orders: 86

**Product-wise performance** showed Vivo and Redmi as the joint top performers, each generating $7M in sales, followed closely by Techno at $6M. Samsung and iPhone trailed behind, each contributing $4M. Despite iPhone typically commanding a premium price point, it did not lead in total sales — suggesting the retailer's volume is driven more by mid-range and budget devices than by premium models.

**City-wise sales** were led by Karachi, accounting for 29.62% of total sales, followed closely by Islamabad (29.36%). Lahore contributed 20.66%, while Sialkot lagged behind at 23.36%. The relatively even split between the top two cities indicates two comparably strong markets, while Lahore represents the smallest share of the four regions.

**Month-wise revenue trends**, filtered to March, show a concentrated revenue spike reaching approximately $28M for that month alone, aligning with the dashboard's total sales figure — indicating March was a particularly strong sales period, potentially tied to a seasonal event or promotional campaign.

**Top 5 salesperson performance** was led by Haseeb at roughly $6.5M in sales, followed by Shahbaz (~$6M), Yasir (~$4.3M), Umair (~$3.8M), and Zeeshan (~$3.3M). The gap between the top two performers and the remaining three suggests a concentration of sales strength in a small group of top salespeople.

## Executive Summary

- The business generated $28M in total sales from 777 units across 86 orders during the period reviewed, with March emerging as a standout revenue month.
- Vivo and Redmi are the strongest-selling products, together accounting for nearly half of total product-wise sales, while iPhone and Samsung underperform relative to the rest of the lineup.
- Karachi and Islamabad are the two leading markets by sales share, together generating close to 59% of total sales, while Lahore is the weakest-performing city.
- Sales performance is concentrated among a small group of top salespeople, with Haseeb and Shahbaz outperforming the rest of the top five by a notable margin.

## Recommendations

The recommendations are organized into three key areas: **Product Strategy**, **Regional Focus**, and **Sales Team Performance**.

**Product Strategy**

- Investigate why iPhone underperforms despite likely higher per-unit pricing; this may reflect pricing sensitivity, limited stock, or weaker promotional support relative to Vivo and Redmi.
- Consider reallocating marketing and inventory investment toward Vivo, Redmi, and Techno, which are already demonstrating strong demand.

**Regional Focus**

- Prioritize sustaining momentum in Karachi and Islamabad, the top two revenue-generating cities, through continued marketing and stock allocation.
- Investigate the underlying causes of Lahore's comparatively weaker sales performance, whether due to market saturation, competition, or distribution gaps, and evaluate whether targeted promotions could close the gap.

**Sales Team Performance**

- Study the sales approach used by top performers Haseeb and Shahbaz to identify best practices that could be replicated across the broader sales team.
- Introduce structured coaching or incentive programs for mid-tier salespeople (Yasir, Umair, Zeeshan) to help close the performance gap with the top two.

In conclusion, a thorough analysis of the sales dashboard data provided valuable insights into product, regional, and salesperson performance. Leveraging these insights and implementing the recommendations above can contribute to stronger, more consistent sales growth.

## Limitations

Although the dashboard provides useful insights, it is subject to a couple of limitations:

- The Month Wise Revenue Trends chart was filtered to a single month (March), limiting visibility into full-year seasonal patterns without additional filtering.
- The dataset does not include cost or profit margin data, which limits the analysis to top-line revenue rather than profitability.
- A more granular breakdown combining product, region, and month simultaneously would help identify which specific product-city-month combinations are driving the strongest results.
